## 1. Overview
Oriv is designed to help you track and understand your daily health metrics and readiness. We place the highest priority on user privacy. Oriv processes your health data **100% locally on your device** via Apple HealthKit. 

We do not operate external cloud servers to store, process, or aggregate your personal biometric data.

---

## 2. HealthKit Data Collection & Usage
Oriv requests read-only authorization from Apple HealthKit for the following health metrics:
* **Heart Rate Variability (HRV - SDNN)**
* **Resting Heart Rate (RHR)**
* **Sleep Analysis**
* **Active Energy Burned**

### How Your Data Is Used
* All calculations (including your daily Readiness Score, rolling 90-day baselines, and Acute-to-Chronic Workload Ratios) are executed strictly on your iPhone.
* Your HealthKit data is used exclusively to display your personal health insight dashboard within the app.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Transmission & Sharing
* **Zero External Telemetry:** Oriv does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share your personal health telemetry with third-party servers, remote databases, or external APIs.
* **No Advertising or Marketing Usage:** In accordance with Apple Developer Guidelines (Guideline 5.1.3), your HealthKit data will never be sold, leased, or transferred to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.

---

## 4. Third-Party Access
Oriv does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or cloud tracking services that harvest personal health data.

---

## 5. Data Control & Deletion
Since all health data remains inside your native Apple Health store, you maintain full control over your permissions:
* You can manage or revoke HealthKit read access at any time on your iPhone via **Settings ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Health ➔ Oriv**.
* Deleting the Oriv application removes all local app caches immediately. Your historical raw health data remains safely stored in your native Apple Health app.
